# Employee User Guide

Welcome to the Staff Work Log & Team Management Web Application. This guide walks you through logging daily deliverables, tracking tasks, and managing work hours.

---

## 1. Signing In
1. Open the application URL in any desktop or mobile browser.
2. Enter your unique **username** and **password** provided by your manager or administrator.
3. Click **Sign In with Secure Session**.

---

## 2. Using the Book-Style Daily Work Log
The daily logbook features a dual-page executive ledger design:

### Left Page: Operations & Deliverables
- **Work Date**: Automatically set to today; can be adjusted if filing a retrospective log.
- **Shift Window**: Enter your shift start time (e.g. `09:00`) and end time (e.g. `17:30`). Total working hours are calculated automatically.
- **Detailed Work Description**: Outline the key accomplishments, client meetings, technical deliverables, or operational tasks executed.
- **Tasks Completed Today**: List finished tickets, deliverable IDs, or milestones reached.
- **Next Working Day's Plan**: State your priorities and goals for tomorrow.

### Right Page: Challenges & Verification
- **Roadblocks & Problems Encountered**: Document any blockers, technical issues, or missing dependencies.
- **Carry-Over / In-Progress Tasks**: Note ongoing tasks that continue into the next day.
- **Additional Notes**: Documentation references, links, or internal meeting details.

### Saving & Submitting
- **Save Draft**: Saves your progress at any time so you can return to it later.
- **Submit Daily Log**: Sends the completed log to your department manager for official approval.
- **Approved Status**: Once approved by your manager, the log receives an official certified seal. If you need to make corrections after approval, request your manager or administrator to reopen the record.

---

## 3. Managing Assigned Tasks
1. Click **Tasks & Gantt Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Toggle between **Table View**, **Kanban Board**, and **Gantt Timeline**.
3. Click any task assigned to you to:
   - Move the **Completion Progress** slider (e.g. 25%, 50%, 100%).
   - Log **Actual Hours** worked.
   - Post comments and progress updates for your team and supervisor.

---

## 4. Notifications & Reminders
- The bell icon in the top header indicates task assignments, manager comments, or approved work logs.
- Click any notification to navigate directly to the relevant task or work log.
